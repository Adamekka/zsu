# Assignment

Source: course handout supplied on 7 November 2026, “Project 1: Exploratory & Cluster Analysis”, lecturer HANO261. Deadline: 15 November 2026 at 23:59.

Submit a Jupyter Notebook with Markdown explanations. It must contain:

- Univariate and bivariate EDA, distributions and correlation matrices. Explain every plot in Markdown.
- Justified handling of missing values and outliers, plus at least two or three new variables based on domain knowledge.
- At least two clustering algorithms, mathematical justification of the cluster count, and real-world interpretations supported by feature comparisons.
- A final `Summary` section with the main EDA and clustering findings.
- Citations following ČSN ISO 690:2022, including the AI models used and how they helped.

The grade emphasizes interpretation of the plots and depth of preprocessing.

The project is individual. AI assistance is permitted, but the student must understand and defend every submitted line, explain methodological choices and algorithm behavior, and explain or modify code during the defense. Czech, Slovak and English are accepted.

Submit original `.ipynb` or `.py` files, without the dataset, PDF or HTML exports. The dataset must be publicly available or previously submitted for selection. Avoid Plotly because of rendering and upload-size issues. The in-person defense is mandatory and in Czech.
